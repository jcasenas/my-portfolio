Overview

SKINETIQUE is a records management system built for a skincare business in Davao City. It digitizes and centralizes the business's client records, treatment history, product inventory, and appointment management — replacing a manual, paper-based workflow.

Built with Laravel and MySQL, the system gives the business owner and staff a clean, organized interface for tracking client data and day-to-day operations.

The Problem

What Needed Solving

Before the implementation of a records management system, SKINETIQUE relied on manual recordkeeping.

This resulted in data inconsistencies and loss of productivity and time.

The Solution

How It Was Built

A proposed UI/UX design and prototype was presented to the client through Figma.

Once the design had been approved, the system was built with the Laravel framework and MySQL (for Database), then later deployed to Render.

Results
